Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026 · This is a mockup page for design purposes and does not reflect a finalized legal document.

Overview

Jigsaw Fun Time! ("we," "our," or "the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the app, you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

  • Photos you choose to use. Images you select from your camera roll or camera are processed to create puzzles and are not shared with third parties beyond what's needed to generate AI images you request.
  • Account and purchase information. If you subscribe or buy a pack, your app store handles payment directly — we receive confirmation of the purchase, not your card details.
  • Gameplay data. Puzzle sizes, completion times, streaks, and credit balances, so your progress and rewards carry across sessions.
  • Device and diagnostic information. Basic device type, OS version, and crash reports, used only to fix bugs and improve performance.

How we use your information

We use collected information to operate the app's core features, personalize your experience, process purchases, respond to support requests, and improve stability and performance over time.

AI-generated images

When you request an AI-generated puzzle image, your prompt and any reference photo are sent to our image-generation provider solely to create that image. We do not use your personal photos to train any AI model.

Advertising

Free-tier users may see ads served through third-party ad networks. These networks may use device identifiers to serve relevant ads. You can remove all ads at any time with the Ad-Free subscription.

Children's privacy

Jigsaw Fun Time! is designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent, in accordance with applicable law.

Your choices

You can request deletion of your account data, opt out of personalized ads through your device settings, and manage or cancel subscriptions directly through your Apple ID or Google Play account.

Contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent through our Support page.
