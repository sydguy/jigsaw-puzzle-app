FAQ

Good to know

Is Jigsaw Fun Time! really free?

Yes. Every puzzle size, your own photos, daily rewards, and both timer modes are free with no sign-up required. Ads may appear between puzzles unless you go Ad-Free.

What happens when I run out of AI credits?

AI credits are used only for AI-generated images — your own photos never require credits. When you run out, you can watch a short rewarded ad for more, or buy an AI Pack for a larger batch.

Can I use my own photos?

Yes — camera roll and live camera photos are free for everyone, with no credit cost at all.

Does the Ad-Free plan remove every ad?

Yes. The $2.99/month Ad-Free subscription removes all ads across the entire app, including between puzzles and on the home screen.

Can I play without an internet connection?

Puzzles you've already generated or saved can be played offline. Generating new AI images and syncing rewards requires a connection.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Ad-Free subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID or Google Play account settings, the same place you manage any other app subscription, and can be cancelled anytime.
