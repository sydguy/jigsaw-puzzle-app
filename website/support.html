Getting started

Setting up the app and creating your first puzzle.

How do I create my first puzzle?

Tap "Add Image" on the home screen, choose a photo from your camera, gallery, or generate one with AI, then pick a puzzle size and difficulty to get started right away.

What puzzle sizes are available?

From a quick 6×8 for a short break up to a 15×20 for a longer session — you can adjust rows and columns independently before starting.

Do I need an account to play?

No account is required to play. Your progress is saved to your device automatically, and syncing across devices is available if you choose to sign in.

Billing & Packs

Subscriptions, AI credits, and one-time purchases.

What's the difference between AI credits and coins?

AI credits are spent only on AI-generated images and come from packs you purchase. Coins are earned for free through daily rewards and rewarded ads, and can be spent on in-puzzle boosts like hints — they're never a substitute for AI credits.

How do I cancel my Ad-Free subscription?

Open your device's App Store or Google Play account settings, find Jigsaw Fun Time! under subscriptions, and cancel there. Access continues until your current billing period ends.

Do AI Packs expire?

No — once purchased, AI Pack credits are yours to use whenever you like, with no expiration date.

I was charged but didn't receive my pack. What do I do?

This is usually resolved by restarting the app to refresh your purchase status. If the issue continues, reach out through the contact form below with your order number.

Account & sync

Keeping your progress safe across devices.

How do I move my progress to a new phone?

Sign in with the same account on your new device before uninstalling the app on your old one, and your puzzles, streaks, and credits will carry over automatically.

Can I delete my account and data?

Yes. Contact support with your request and we'll remove your account data in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

Troubleshooting

Common fixes for crashes and image issues.

The app crashed while generating an AI image. Was I charged?

No — credits are only deducted after an image successfully generates. If a generation fails, your credit is automatically refunded.

My puzzle pieces aren't loading correctly.

Try closing and reopening the app first. If the issue persists, check that you have the latest version installed from the App Store or Google Play.
